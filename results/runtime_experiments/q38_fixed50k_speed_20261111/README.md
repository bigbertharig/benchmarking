# Qwen3.8 Flash-Next Q4: fixed 50K long-output speed comparison (2026-11-11)

This experiment held one saved [49,694-token conversation fixture](fixed_50k_chat.json) constant across six cold requests. The fixture SHA-256 is `c933220125cf0100cca2a29e59e499a62dcc78609be7b77e5de2ba9fff9f151f`. Each run used the same UD-Q4_K_XL pack on NVMe, Strata 0.1.56 CUDA12, GPU0 (3090 Ti), one 131,072-token slot, temperature 0, and a 768-token output limit. The four control runs alternated INT8 and K8V4 with 12 GiB of resident expert RAM; the final two changed the K8V4 resident budget to 16 GiB or enabled the model's MTP draft head. GPU1–5 remained idle. The full [sequence receipt](sequence.json), individual `terminal.json` receipts, engine logs, and runner scripts are retained here and in `scripts/integration/`.

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| INT8 / 12 GiB A | 712.9 | 713.0 | 7.7 | 813.3 | 89.3% | 346.5 |
| K8V4 / 12 GiB A | 709.3 | 709.4 | 6.2 | 833.3 | 86.5% | 352.9 |
| INT8 / 12 GiB B | 713.6 | 713.8 | 6.0 | 840.6 | 86.9% | 356.5 |
| K8V4 / 12 GiB B | 719.7 | 719.8 | 7.3 | 824.8 | 88.2% | 349.4 |
| K8V4 / **16 GiB** | **659.6** | **659.7** | 6.2 | **783.3** | 88.0% | **329.5** |
| K8V4 / 12 GiB / **MTP** | 783.2 | 783.7 | 6.1 | 908.8 | 85.6% | 389.7 |

All six generated exactly 768 tokens and ended at the output limit. The two INT8 runs spanned 6.0–7.7 tok/s, and the two K8V4 runs 6.2–7.3 tok/s. Their prefill times were much tighter at 709–720 seconds. **These repeats do not establish a KV-format speed winner.** The 16 GiB resident expert budget cut K8V4 prefill by 50–60 seconds and total request time by 42–50 seconds versus the two 12 GiB K8V4 controls; it also reduced host NVMe reads by 20–23 GB. This is one 16 GiB sample, so repeat it before adopting it as a default. Host `MemAvailable` after that load was about 8 GiB; swap use rose during the run.

The valid MTP arm used a separately fetched and hash-verified draft layer. Strata's Q2 draft runtime was built on the rig; the [live engine command](mtp_direct/terminal.json) included `--mtp .../mtp/rt`. It accepted **387 of 549** drafted tokens (70.5%), but its decode rate was 6.1 tok/s and its complete request took 909 seconds. The draft head reduced the GPU expert cache from 5,469 to 5,147 slots, and this run had the lowest decode hit rate and highest NVMe reads of the series. The larger expert traffic and smaller cache are plausible reasons the draft did not help, but one MTP sample does not isolate causation. Its stored [engine log](mtp_direct/engine.log) reports 425.7 GB of file-tier OS reads, 450.1 GB of expert reads, and 132.5 seconds of decode fetch wait; those are engine counters with different scopes from the host NVMe block counter in the table.

The first MTP launcher attempt was aborted during prefill and excluded because `--mtp on` did **not** add an MTP path to the live engine command, despite the launch plan asking for a 5.2 GB draft download. Its [attempt receipt](mtp_followup/sequence.json) and the guarded [second attempt](mtp_verified/sequence.json) document the mismatch. Strata setup could not complete on the rig because its GitHub DNS check failed. The Pi fetched only the pinned MTP tensors, Strata's `verify` command exited 0, and the raw 4.9 GB staging is retained outside Git at `/media/bryan/shared/model-staging/qwen38-mtp-bf16-20261111`. The compact MTP GGUF and runtime files stay on the rig NVMe under the model's `runtime/strata-ud-q4_k_xl/mtp/` directory. The valid MTP arm used an experiment-specific `strata serve --profile` config rather than changing the saved launcher profile.

**Measurement boundaries.** “First streamed content” is the first nonempty content or reasoning SSE chunk, not a hidden internal token timestamp. Prefill and decode rates come from the engine's per-request line. NVMe reads are host-wide `/sys/block/nvme0n1/stat` deltas for the complete request, not expert-only bytes or separate prefill/decode byte counts. The saved fixture is one long user message, not a multi-turn exchange or a KV snapshot; every arm cold-prefilled it with zero KV reuse. Model load time (about 98–125 seconds per arm) is excluded from request wall time. This is a speed study, not a scored quality or long-context recall evaluation. No output text was retained for answer-quality review.

**Next probes.** Repeat 12 versus 16 GiB K8V4 in alternating order, with phase-separated NVMe counters and a warm continuation on the same 50K chat. Profile why MTP raises expert I/O despite accepting 70% of drafts, then consider smaller draft windows or more expert RAM only if the trace shows a plausible end-to-end gain. The final verified MTP service was left loaded on GPU0; see its [loaded-state receipt](final_loaded.json) before any new run. This service was started directly through `strata serve --profile`, so the launch controller may not own its stop lifecycle; its serve PID is in the [MTP terminal receipt](mtp_direct/terminal.json) for a controlled change of loads.
